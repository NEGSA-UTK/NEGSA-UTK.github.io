About

Grad school is easier with people in it.

NEGSA exists so that no one in this department has to figure things out alone — the qualifying exam, the advisor conversation, the job search, the move to Knoxville. We organize the things that make a department feel like a place rather than a building.

We also carry student concerns to the department head and the graduate studies office. If something in the program isn't working, tell us and we'll raise it.

  • Representation

    A standing channel between grad students and department leadership on funding, policy, and program changes.

  • Professional development

    Résumé nights, mock quals, and talks from Oak Ridge, Y-12, and the hundred-plus nuclear companies within an hour of campus.

  • Community

    Socials, intramurals, and a welcome for every incoming cohort — including the international students landing in Tennessee for the first time.

  • Outreach

    Volunteering at department tours and K–12 events, because someone did that for us first.
